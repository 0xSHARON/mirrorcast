RFC 6762 / mDNS Zeroconf Multicast

The Windows receiver broadcasts its presence on multicast address 224.0.0.251:5353. The Android client resolves receiver hostname and WebSocket port without user intervention.

Target: MirrorCast-Desktop._mirrorcast._tcp.local
IPv4: 192.168.1.42:8080 • TXT: v=1.0.0;auth=pin;hw=d3d11

Dual Transport Pipeline

Automatic zero-configuration Wi-Fi mDNS pairing, or fallback to zero-jitter USB ADB reverse tunneling (tcp:8080) without requiring Wi-Fi.
